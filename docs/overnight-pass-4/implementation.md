## Playability decisions

A .47-second phone contact preview is too brief to choose a weapon and aim from scratch. Prepared action needs a clear control and a steadier view. The existing workshop has flight weapons (aim/click, 1–3 selection, F); it does not currently expose directional airborne steering. No steering binding or extra physical boost was invented for this pass.

- Added a 48-pixel-tall Fire button inside the existing weapon tray, equivalent to F: hit the current critter with the selected weapon. Pointer aiming remains available. The button names the selected weapon, disables for pre-release/finished runs, exhausted ammo and staff overheat, and preserves existing cooldown/ammo/heat/impulse behavior. Hit flashes now follow the critter after the camera updates, fixing flashes drifting into the HUD during release acquisition. Manual QA verified Slingshot button/F parity, Staff heat, Air 1→0 ammo and disabled Fire. These are controls needed for the current action, not automatic hints or basket guidance.
- Landing anticipation eases active playback toward .62 only for a substantial approaching solid landing. It has a finite budget, recovers after the approach, respects pause/speed controls and never assigns force, velocity or body angle. Release and final physical outcomes remain exact in the unarmed parity regression. Tiny contact aftermath is not slowed. This adds roughly .38/1.40/2.17 presentation seconds across weak/medium/full natural runs; physics time does not change.
- The camera holds its local open lens across a rebound chain for 1.65 seconds, uses a 4% opening deadband and recovers slowly. Target-ID confirmation/dropout grace and bounded pan rates prevent raw forecast jitter from panning the view. The context size floor rises from 4.5% to 5.2%: minimum 20.28-pixel body length at 390 px width, versus 17.79–18.90 px previously. Release acquisition stays .55 seconds; all scales remain physical and reduced motion remains centered.

On the same cached physical trajectories, phone zoom log-total-variation drops 73% / 92% / 83% for weak / medium / full; significant zoom reversals drop 3→0 / 11→0 / 9→2. Camera-only main full contact previews lose at most 33 ms to preserve a larger critter. With the new bounded tempo, actual presentation contact previews in the controlled full run are .750/.783/.867/.800 seconds, versus .483/.517/.533/.517 before. This supports an already-selected action; it is not proof that an unfamiliar player can make a fresh choice and aim in that window. See camera-comfort.json and playability.json.
