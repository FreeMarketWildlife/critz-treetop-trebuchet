# Slower, weightier swing

`launch-feel.js` advances the existing fixed-step solver through a presentation clock. The small starter ramps from 0.26 to 0.50 of normal simulation pace during the swing. Its 0.525-second physical swing now lasts roughly 1.375 seconds on screen (2.6× longer), while the actual measured release remains 39.07955445° at 21.99685501 m/s. Larger rigs get a less extreme slowdown. A stalled combination recovers normal pacing between four and six playback seconds. The speed selector multiplies playback; it is now labelled Playback speed.

At separation a 45 ms beat holds the world, then flight eases back to full pace over 280 ms. Meaningful impacts receive a 25–50 ms beat, at most once per 200 ms; leaves and sap cannot trigger it. This timing changes neither solver forces, geometry nor release energy. The module calls ordinary `sim.step` with a smaller budget and uses the same fixed internal timestep. No pose or velocity is assigned. Existing shop, saving, map editing, point-specific loading, guide rail and crank direction remain.

Original sound cues build from a low wood creak to a rising sling swish and a short marimba-like release pop alongside the launch sound. Cues only run after user audio unlock, use the bounded voice pool and stop on pause. The setup camera pushes in gently (4.5%) during the swing. Counterweight ghost points trace its actual recent positions. The release draws a short golden fan at the real separation point, with a small camera impulse; impacts draw a brief ring. Camera movement is capped at 2.3 horizontal / 1.5 vertical pixels. Reduced motion suppresses the push, ghosts, streaks and shake in favor of a stationary ring. Feedback uses active playback time, so pauses and dialogs freeze it; paused feedback does not keep rendering indefinitely. Rope display integration now follows physical simulation time, including a genuine zero-time freeze, so it cannot continue sagging during hit pause.

Validation: 401 full-suite tests pass. Six pacing/feedback regressions compare exact release time, angle, speed, energy and velocity with the unchanged baseline, across all five foundations and a whale-scale rig; they also cover paused state, speed control, bounded contact pause, quiet foliage/sap, stalled recovery and zero-time rope inertia. The original audio envelope/voice-pool tests cover all three new cues. Local phone-width playtesting verified manual drop → wind → slower swing → release; the DOM measured 1.374 seconds of swing. Pausing held the crank and presentation time unchanged, and the release effect was inspected while paused. Screenshots are retained in this folder. These are browser and numerical checks, not physical-device FPS or listening-panel certification.

The pre-existing `docs/custom-workshop/public-game.png` is retained as an archived screenshot of the earlier release; it is not evidence of this version.
