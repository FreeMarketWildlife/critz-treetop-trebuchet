# Treetop weapon art bible — Toss the Turtle direction

Use this before adding or revising weapon art. Reference: Phil’s October 20 screenshot and [Toss the Turtle](https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/508440), by Foreverkul and Gonzossm. Original Critz designs come from [the supplied weapon deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1pJI_tjgTwgB5d42orHLOi7KgTX1_u-YOIgdsae2DZ7o/edit). Do not reuse the reference game’s sprites.

## Visual rules

- Thick, almost black warm outlines, rounded joins, slightly irregular silhouettes. At 120 px art size use a 4.5 px outer stroke and 2 px inner marks.
- Large, readable primary shapes: wide slingshot fork, oversized crystal, fat bottle belly. Keep identifying details visible at 40–60 px.
- Flat saturated fills, one hard shadow shape and one light shape. No photorealism, soft painted texture, tiny etched patterns, metallic gradients, or generic glowing UI icons.
- Wood: ochre/tan highlights and brown shadows. Crystal: cyan, ice-blue facet, deep blue shadow. Air bottle: pale mint glass and dark teal silhouette.
- Punchy impact poses and jagged comic bursts. Short THWIP / ZAP / WHOOSH lettering has dark outlines and cream fill. Effects stop when paused.
- HUD echoes the reference’s compact upper-left weapon/ammo stack. Weapons have illustrated silhouettes and remaining shots; avoid large inventory panels over the flight view.
- Preserve Critz identities and existing volunteer art. No humans or operators. This pass applies these rules to weapons, not a replacement of the whole environment.

Deck damage, fire rate and magazines describe its source game. Treetop shot counts, hit areas, heat and velocity changes are explicit game balance adaptations, not additional world canon. All three are immediately available in this initial set. No shop prices or progression gates are introduced.

## Acceptance

Inspect the actual running game at desktop and portrait widths. Silhouettes must remain distinct, counts legible and controls reachable. Clicks near the critter hit; misses consume ammunition. Staff heat locks and recovers. Reset replenishes the loadout. Pause/modal/blur cannot fire. Only flight can receive weapon impulses; loading and the gravity-driven mechanism must remain unaffected.
