# Catalogue visual audit

This audit covers all **48 parts in 12 slots**, from the actual persisted IDs in `src/build-catalog.js`. It concerns equipped mechanism rendering. Shop card illustrations and the effects of an upgrade on range are separate questions. No physical dimensions, fixture shapes, strengths, prices, ownership, currency or saves were changed by this renderer pass.

Capacity upgrades retain the player's selected dimensions/fill. The new surface treatments identify the equipped tier; they do not imply that buying capacity automatically increases mass, scale or throwing distance. Colours, weave, coatings, fittings and cages are decorative, while the actual frame members, rope endpoints, pin endpoint, hinge, counterweight radius and body transforms still come from the simulation.

`test/catalogue-art.test.js` records the real renderer's Canvas commands for every catalogue entry, at identical compatible dimensions. It checks pairwise distinctions within every slot even with fill/stroke colours excluded, finite geometry, balanced Canvas state, unchanged simulation snapshots, and bounded command counts. It also covers all six physical loading-basket fabrics, all 48 entries at 390×844 phone, 300×220 workshop preview and 1726×875 desktop sizes, and surface/pivot attachment on translated/leaning frames. The four focused tests pass.

These are source/rendering regression checks. They do not certify human recognition of every small fastener on a phone, aesthetic quality, frame rate on a real device, or a range benefit for every swap. Native desktop/phone review of the integrated build remains necessary. In particular, review the starter's additional cage bands for clutter and the axle cap's visibility at its small real pivot before accepting the visual pass.
