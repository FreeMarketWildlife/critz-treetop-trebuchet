Critz: Treetop Trebuchet

We're going places. • Local physics prototype • Infinite health, harmless slapstick

How to load and launchFirst set cocking height: a torque-limited motor physically winds the counterweight. Then drag the articulated critter over the green cradle and let it drop in. Contacts and gravity seat it; no radial snap or settling timer. The drag spring has an 80 N limit. Optional tap-to-hold keeps that same force-driven grip until a second tap. Unlatch releases the physical arm from the finite frame. The meter sets a reachable winding target, not a pose or speed bonus. Motor work and actual height remain simulation outputs. The dotted hint fades before first obstacles. After launch, arrows provide small arcade nudges; for flying squirrels, right dives and left climbs using lift/drag.
