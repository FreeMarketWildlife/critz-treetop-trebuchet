How to load and launchHold the winding button (or focus it and hold Space/Enter), then release near the middle of its meter; the slider also accepts a direct target. A torque-limited motor physically winds the counterweight. Pause or hiding this tab freezes simulation time. Lift the critter straight up beside the green cradle first. Move across only when the whole critter is above its floor, wait for it to catch up, then release your grip and let it drop in. Pulling straight through the cradle from below hits its solid floor. Contacts and gravity seat it; no radial snap or settling timer. The drag spring has a mass-scaled force limit (shown when capped). Optional tap-to-hold keeps that same force-driven grip until a second tap. Unlatch releases the physical arm from the finite frame. The split pouch has a permanent hinge stop against inward collapse; loss of strap support lets its panel fold outward. The meter sets a reachable winding target, not a pose or speed bonus. Motor work and actual height remain simulation outputs. The dotted hint fades before first obstacles. After launch, arrows provide small arcade nudges; for flying squirrels, right dives and left climbs using lift/drag.
